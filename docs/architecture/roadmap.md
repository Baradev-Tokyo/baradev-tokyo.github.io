# Roadmap

1. Foundation: SEO audit and metadata, analytics contracts, billing sandbox, adapter interfaces and deterministic cost fixtures.
2. Owner milestone: finish Stripe Japan onboarding review and test hosted Checkout and signed webhook delivery with a sandbox. Verify Search Console and GA4 in owner accounts. Confirm private hosting, legal disclosures and cost ceilings before moving beyond local.
3. Productize maintenance and prepaid hosting first. Add managed database, private runtime, tests against PostgreSQL, backup recovery, and owner inbox/billing runbook before exposing workflows.
4. Import read-only cloud and marketing data. Add approved content drafts and capped background jobs. Enable write adapters individually after provider approval.
5. Add subscription entitlement and customer workspace membership, tenant isolation and audit tests before SaaS access. Add AI authoring and optional video rendering as independently approved jobs.

For marketing, keep this lifecycle separate from the lead inbox: Research → draft → quality and policy validation → owner approval → scheduled publication → performance tracking → optimization. Only the offline stages are available before provider approval. Prefer a standardized maintenance/reporting pilot with recurring scope and explicit limits over expanding consulting.
