# Japanese keyword opportunities

Search result observations from November 2026 establish vocabulary and result types. They do not establish volume, rank, conversion, or difficulty. Validate queries and country segments in Search Console after deployment and treat service estimates as hypotheses until inquiries arrive.

| Cluster | Japanese starting queries | Intent and first content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Python development | Python 開発 外注、Python 受託開発 東京 | Vendor selection. Explain integrations, data processing and handoff on the services page. |
| Django | Django 受託開発、Django Webアプリ 開発 | Framework-led procurement. Explain Django delivery and maintenance when portfolio evidence is ready. |
| Booking | 予約システム 開発、予約管理システム 事前決済 | Compare off-the-shelf and bespoke booking. Use Pedal Somewhere as verified case evidence. |

| Marketing | 中小企業 マーケティング 自動化、問い合わせ対応 自動化 | Problem research and vendor discovery. Start with a concrete workflow. Avoid vague AI promises. |

Observed vocabulary: [Japanese system development service and Stripe/LINE integrations](https://minister.co.jp/system-development), [official LINE booking case](https://www.lycbiz.com/sites/default/files/media/jp/case-study/dl-pdf/spice-works.pdf), [published Japanese maintenance offer](https://iron-web.jp/maintenance/), and [Google's Japanese review-management wording](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474050?hl=ja). These sources validate terminology only. The proposed long-tail phrases, conversion intent, and Tokyo modifier remain hypotheses.

Prioritize booking and Stripe case studies, then maintenance. Review query impressions, CTR, package selections, and confirmed inquiries monthly. Avoid publishing near-duplicate pages per keyword. Add Japanese and English counterparts only when the content genuinely matches.
