# Japan launch checklist

- **Specified Commercial Transactions Act:** Determine whether direct online service sales are covered. Where applicable, review the Tokushoho disclosure and ordering confirmation screens before publication. Confirm legal name, applicable disclosures, charges, delivery timing and cancellation terms. Keep placeholders in the [drafts](../legal-drafts.md) private until approved. [CAA guidance](https://www.no-trouble.caa.go.jp/what/mailorder/)
- **APPI and privacy:** Map contact, analytics, provider and account data. Document purpose, data minimization, retention, access, deletion and cross-border processor arrangements. Record consent and honor withdrawal where relevant. Keep inquiries, credentials, identities and customer data out of Git and marketplace screening inputs. [PPC guidance](https://www.ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/faq/APPI_QA/)
- **Invoices and tax:** Verify consumption-tax status, accounting treatment and whether registration as a qualified invoice issuer is appropriate. Confirm invoice format with a Japanese tax professional. Never invent a registration number or treat receipts as automatically qualified. [National Tax Agency](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shohi/6498.htm)
- **Electronic marketing:** Separate operational messages from advertising. Require approved opt-in and suppression evidence for future campaigns, a working unsubscribe route and required sender details. Review applicability before any email campaigns. [Japanese anti-spam guidance](https://www.dekyo.or.jp/soudan/data/taisaku/201807mlp.pdf)
- **Platform verification:** Use existing marketplace accounts. The owner supplies real identity, payout and applicable tax details directly on each platform. Do not assume a Japanese seller needs another jurisdiction's tax form. Track marketplace income, fees, taxes and exchange rates separately. [Fiverr publishing requirements](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010451397-Creating-a-Gig) · [Upwork support](https://support.upwork.com/)
