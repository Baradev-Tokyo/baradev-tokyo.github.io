## Terms of service draft

The signed proposal or agreement specifies services, milestones, dependencies, acceptance, price, payment dates and support. Changes to scope require prior written agreement. Describe intellectual property and licenses, confidentiality, third-party accounts and costs, support response windows, and what happens when the engagement ends. **Owner decision required:** final allocation of IP, liability, governing terms, and precedence between this page and the executed agreement.

## Refund and cancellation draft

For one-off work, show cancellation terms before payment and describe how already completed work and preapproved expenses are accounted for. For recurring work, state renewal dates, cancellation method and deadline, when service access ends, and when refunds apply. **Owner decision required:** exact deadlines, fee treatment, expected refund processing and handling of required statutory rights before accepting orders.
