# Incident response

1. Record timestamp, source and correlation ID. Do not copy customer content or secrets into logs.
2. Pause the relevant local job or disable its provider gate. Inspect the private dashboard and provider status. Revoke leaked credentials promptly and rotate authorized secrets.
3. Review leases and provider idempotency before retrying. Check ambiguous payments or email submissions directly before resending. Keep platform-origin contracts and payment disputes inside their platform.
4. For privacy incidents, preserve necessary evidence and review APPI notification duties with the owner. Test restoration from backups offline. Never delete customer production data or suspend access as automatic recovery.
5. Re-enable only after confirming scope, cost and approvals. Update the audit record and prevention checklist.
